Recursive mounts
When you bind mount a path that itself contains mounts, those submounts are
also included in the bind mount by default. This behavior is configurable,
using the
bind-recursive
option for
--mount
. This option is only supported
with the
--mount
flag, not with
-v
or
--volume
.
