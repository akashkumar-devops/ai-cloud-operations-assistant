Mounting a volume over existing data
If you mount a
non-empty volume
into a directory in the container in which
files or directories exist, the pre-existing files are obscured by the mount.
This is similar to if you were to save files into
/mnt
on a Linux host, and
then mounted a USB drive into
/mnt
