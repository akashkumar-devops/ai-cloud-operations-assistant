Block IO weight (relative weight) accepts a weight value between 10 and 1000.
--blkio-weight-device=""
Block IO weight (relative device weight, format:
DEVICE_NAME:WEIGHT
)
--device-read-bps=""
Limit read rate from a device (format:
<device-path>:<number>[<unit>]
). Number is a positive integer. Unit can be one of
kb
,
mb
, or
gb
.
--device-write-bps=""
Limit write rate to a device (format:
<device-path>:<number>[<unit>]
). Number is a positive integer. Unit can be one of
kb
,
mb
, or
gb
.
