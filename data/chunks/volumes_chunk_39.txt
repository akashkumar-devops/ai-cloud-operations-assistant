Options for --mount
The
--mount
flag consists of multiple key-value pairs, separated by commas
and each consisting of a
<key>=<value>
tuple. The order of the keys isn't
significant.
$
docker run --mount
type
=
volume
[
,src
=
<volume-name>
]
,dst
=
<mount-path>
[
,<key>
=
<value>...
]
